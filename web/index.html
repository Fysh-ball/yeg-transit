    Edmonton bus times for the stops you use

    YEG Bus shows when the next Edmonton Transit Service bus or LRT train reaches your stop, counting down in minutes from live GPS data. Save the stops by home, work or school once and they open first every time, with no account and no searching. Pick only the routes you ride, set your walk to the stop and it tells you when to leave, and watch a transfer to see if your connection is on track.

    When your phone has no data, saved stops still show the timetable, and a button texts the stop number to ETS's 31100 service for live times. When ETS closes a stop for construction, the card tells you and offers the replacement stop. Turn a tablet sideways for a wall board by the front door, or add the Android widget to your home screen.
