Privacy

Last updated 22 October 2026 (ads added). Short version: no accounts, and nothing about you is kept on our server. The website shows Google ads, and Google uses cookies for them.

What stays on your phone

Your saved stops, groups, walk times and settings are kept in your browser (or the Android app) on your own device. We never see them. Clearing the site's data or removing the stop deletes them.

What reaches our server

To show times, your device asks our server for a stop's departures. When you search, the words you type go to our server, which looks them up for you. When you tap "Near me", your device shares your location with our server once to find the closest stops. Our server does not log requests and does not save your location or searches.

We look up addresses (Photon) and weather (Open-Meteo) from our server, so those services never see you.

Other services your device talks to

  • Cloudflare carries the traffic to our server and may keep its own short-term logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • When you open the map, map tiles load from OpenStreetMap and the map code loads from cdnjs (Cloudflare). They see your IP address, like any website you visit.

Visit counts

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and how fast pages load. A small script reports the page you opened, how long it took, your browser type and your country. It sets no cookies and doesn't follow you to other sites.

Ads and cookies

The website shows ads from Google AdSense, at the sides on a computer and at the bottom on a phone. The Android app, its widget and the wall board have no ads. We set no cookies ourselves.

Google and its partners use cookies and similar technology to show ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads in Google's My Ad Center, or opt out of many ad companies at aboutads.info. Google explains how it uses this data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
